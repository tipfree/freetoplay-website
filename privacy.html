This Privacy Policy explains how FreeToPlay (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you use our mobile application and related services.

FreeToPlay is a free-to-play app where users can earn virtual coins, play minigames, and redeem eligible winnings for real rewards such as PayPal credit, Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other available reward options. The app is funded primarily through advertisements.

1.Personal Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data.

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Account creation date
  • Login provider information, for example if you sign in with Google
  • Authentication-related data

If we use third-party authentication providers such as Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In, your authentication credentials are processed by those providers. We do not store your password in plain text.

1.2 Guest Account Information

If you use the app as a guest, we may create an anonymous user ID to store your coins, game activity, rewards, and progress. If you later create a full account, we may link your guest account data to your registered account.

1.3 Gameplay and Transaction Data

To operate the app, we collect information about your in-app activity, including:

  • Coins earned
  • Coins spent
  • Minigames played
  • Game results
  • Rewarded ads watched
  • Daily rewards claimed
  • Promo codes or referral codes used
  • Reward requests
  • Payout status
  • Balance history
  • In-app transactions
  • Feedback submitted by you

This data is necessary to provide the app, calculate balances correctly, prevent abuse, and process reward requests.

1.4 Payout and Reward Data

If you request a payout or reward, we may collect and process data required to complete the reward, such as:

  • Selected payout method
  • PayPal email address
  • Cryptocurrency wallet address, for example a Bitcoin Lightning or Ethereum address
  • Requested payout amount
  • Payout status
  • Transaction references
  • Fraud prevention checks
  • Communication related to the payout

Please make sure that payout information you provide is accurate. Incorrect payout details may lead to failed or delayed payouts.

1.5 Advertising Data

Because FreeToPlay is funded by advertisements, we and our advertising partners may process advertising-related data, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • App usage events
  • Rewarded ad views
  • Ad interaction data
  • In-app segment information, such as your VIP status level and account type
  • Approximate location, depending on ad network settings and consent
  • Ad revenue estimates
  • Ad callback data used to verify rewarded ads
  • Consent status for personalized or non-personalized ads

This data may be processed by advertising networks such as Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, Unity or other mediation and advertising partners used in the app.

1.6 Device and Technical Data

We may collect technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language settings
  • Country or region
  • IP address
  • Crash logs
  • Performance data
  • Security and diagnostic data

This data helps us keep the app secure, fix bugs, improve performance, and detect suspicious activity.

1.7 Usage Data

We may collect information about how you interact with the app, including:

  • Screens visited
  • Buttons clicked
  • Session duration
  • Features used
  • Reward and payout activity
  • General user behavior inside the app

We use this information to improve the app, understand user behavior, and optimize the user experience.

1.8 Communication Data

If you contact us, send feedback, or request support, we may process:

  • Your email address
  • Your message content
  • Support history
  • Technical information needed to solve your issue

2.How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes.

2.1 Providing the App

We process your data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Save your coin balance
  • Enable minigame participation
  • Track wins and losses
  • Process rewarded ads
  • Enable daily rewards, promo codes, and referrals
  • Process payout and reward requests
  • Provide customer support

2.2 Reward and Payout Processing

We use payout-related data to:

  • Verify your eligibility for rewards
  • Process payout requests
  • Prevent duplicate or fraudulent payouts
  • Communicate with you about reward status
  • Keep necessary transaction records

2.3 Advertising and Monetization

We use advertising-related data to:

  • Show rewarded ads
  • Verify completed ad views
  • Credit coins after eligible ad interactions
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent ad reward abuse
  • Serve personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent and settings

2.4 Fraud Prevention and Security

Because FreeToPlay offers real rewards, we process certain data to protect the app against abuse, including:

  • Fake accounts
  • Bot activity
  • Duplicate accounts
  • Manipulated ad views
  • Manipulated game results
  • Suspicious payout requests
  • Abuse of promo codes or referral systems
  • Technical attacks or unauthorized access

If we detect suspicious activity, we may temporarily restrict or permanently block accounts, deny payouts, or request additional verification.

2.5 App Improvement and Analytics

We may use data to:

  • Improve app stability
  • Understand which features are used most
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Improve game balancing
  • Optimize reward systems
  • Improve user experience

2.6 Communication

We may use your email address to send:

  • Account-related messages
  • Security notifications
  • Payout confirmations
  • Important app updates
  • Customer support replies
  • Changes to our Privacy Policy or Terms

These messages are service-related and are not marketing emails.

2.7 Marketing Emails

If you give us your consent, we may use your email address to send you marketing communications, including:

  • Bonus campaigns
  • Promotions
  • New minigame announcements
  • Reward opportunities
  • Special events
  • App updates and offers

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us at freetoplay.business@gmail.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before the withdrawal.

We may still send you non-marketing emails that are necessary for your account, security, payouts, or service operation.

3.Legal Basis for Processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data based on the following legal bases.

3.1 Performance of a Contract — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

We process data where it is necessary to provide the app and its core functions, including:

  • Account creation
  • Gameplay
  • Coin balance management
  • Reward processing
  • Payout requests
  • Customer support

3.2 Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR

We rely on your consent for certain processing activities, including:

  • Marketing emails
  • Personalized advertising, where required
  • Use of advertising identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies, where required
  • Optional analytics, where required

You may withdraw consent at any time.

3.3 Legal Obligation — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR

We may process and retain certain data where required by law, for example:

  • Accounting obligations
  • Tax requirements
  • Legal claims
  • Regulatory requests
  • Fraud investigation records

3.4 Legitimate Interests — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR

We may process data based on our legitimate interests, including:

  • Fraud prevention
  • App security
  • Abuse detection
  • Service improvement
  • Debugging and crash analysis
  • Protecting our rights and users
  • Preventing unauthorized access

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we balance our interests against your rights and freedoms.

4.Advertising, Consent, and Third-Party Ad Partners

FreeToPlay uses advertising to fund the app and provide free gameplay and rewards.

We may work with advertising partners such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Other ad networks or mediation partners

These partners may collect or receive data from your device, including advertising identifiers, device information, app interaction data, and ad performance data.

Where required in your region, for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, a consent dialog is shown before personalized ads are served. Where this consent dialog is available, you can change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time via Options > Ad Privacy in the app, or through your device’s advertising settings.

  • Fraud prevention services

We do not sell your personal data. We may share personal data in the following situations:

6.1 Service Providers

We may share data with providers who help us operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, analytics, advertising, email, support, and payout providers.

6.2 Advertising Partners

Advertising partners may receive data necessary to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and verify rewarded ad views.

6.3 Payout Providers

If you request a reward, we may share the necessary payout details with the relevant payout provider.

6.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, public authority, or legal process.

6.5 Protection of Rights and Security

We may disclose data where necessary to detect fraud, prevent abuse, protect users, enforce our terms, or protect our legal rights.

Some of our service providers may process personal data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area.

8.Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Retention periods may depend on the type of data:

  • Account data is generally retained while your account exists.
  • Gameplay and coin transaction data may be retained to keep balances accurate and prevent fraud.
  • Payout records may be retained for legal, accounting, tax, dispute, and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Marketing email consent records may be retained as proof of consent.
  • Support messages may be retained as long as needed to handle your request and protect our rights.
  • Technical logs may be retained for a limited period unless longer retention is required for security or fraud prevention.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it.

9.Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data. We provide account deletion options:

  • Inside the app: Options > Delete Account
  • Outside the app: free-to-play.net/delete

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, unless we are required or permitted to retain certain data.

We may retain limited data where necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Accounting or tax requirements
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Payout disputes
  • Enforcement of our Terms
  • Prevention of duplicate or abusive accounts

After deletion, you may lose access to your coins, progress, rewards, payout history, and account-related features.
