FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Is FreeToPlay really free?

Completely. The app is funded by advertisements, so you never deposit money and there is nothing to buy. Every coin you wager started as a free reward.

How do I earn coins?

Four ways: watch short rewarded videos (the coin amount varies per video), claim your daily bonus, invite friends (+100 coins per referral, for both of you), and win minigames.

What do payouts cost?

$2 = 3,600 coins, $5 = 8,250, $10 = 15,000 and $20 = 25,000. VIP tiers discount those prices by up to 10%, and you can redeem up to $20 per week.

How do payouts arrive?

PayPal payouts go to the e-mail registered on your account. Bitcoin payouts are processed exclusively over the Lightning Network — we'll e-mail you and ask you to send us a Lightning invoice for your payout amount. For Ethereum, we'll e-mail you to confirm the wallet address before sending. All requests run through standard fraud-prevention checks first, so make sure your contact details are accurate.

Who can play?

FreeToPlay is for adults aged 18 and over. Additional eligibility requirements may apply depending on your region — see our Terms for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Or do it online any time via our account deletion page. Details on what happens to your data are in our Privacy Policy.
